About Me

I'm Osama, a front-end web developer who also works as a visual designer. On the code side I build responsive, fast-loading websites with clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript. On the design side I create scroll-stopping social media ads and YouTube thumbnails that help creators and brands get more clicks.

I like sitting at the crossover of development and design — a site should look good and work well, and a thumbnail or ad should look good and earn the click.
